On processors with efficency cores, do the following to get full performance on Windows:

.\powercfg.exe /powerthrottling disable /path 'C:\Program Files\Oracle\VirtualBox\VBoxHeadless.exe'
.\powercfg.exe /powerthroattling disable /path 'C:\Program Files\Oracle\VirtualBox\VirtualBoxVM.exe'
bcdedit /set hypervisorlaunchtype off
powercfg /powerthrottling list

To fix slow IOPs on windows:
Disable "Core isolation","Memory integrity"
